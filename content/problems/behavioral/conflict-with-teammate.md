## Description

Tell me about a time you had a significant disagreement with a teammate or stakeholder.

# Interviewer Context

Useful follow-up areas:

- What specifically caused the disagreement?
- What was the candidate's own position and responsibility?
- How did they seek to understand the other perspective?
- How was the disagreement resolved?
- What was the outcome for the team or product?
- What would the candidate do differently now?

Probe for a concrete example and distinguish the candidate's actions from the team's collective work. Do not invent missing details.
